Walk Challenges

Two games built out of the walk — and out of the data I logged while walking it.

Growing up, I always loved coding games with Scratch, and so when I had the opportunity to create some challenges for this walk, I was extremely excited to do so! I present “Quiz Time!” — a Climate Walk Trivia challenge based directly on the expert interviews (many thanks to them!!), and “The Letter Collection Game” — where you can brave the sidewalks to complete the NYC to DC trek, helping spread the word about climate and weather science along the way. Will you make it?! Let’s find out... (You got this!!)

How this works. Every line on the left is the English as it appears on the walk page; the box on the right is the machine draft in your language. Correct anything that reads wrong, then tick the box (or press Ctrl+Enter in a box) to mark it checked. Your work saves in this browser as you type. When you are done — or just done for today — press Download corrected file and send that file to Syl; it drops straight into the site. A box you leave empty shows the English on the site, which is the safe choice if you are unsure. Names of people and places are best left as they are.

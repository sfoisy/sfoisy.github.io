What this does not do

No cookies, no fingerprint, no identifier, so no consent banner and nothing for a reader to click past. That is a deliberate trade: you get views and referring hosts, and you do not get unique visitors, sessions, or where anyone went next. If you ever need those, the honest step up is a privacy-first analytics service rather than more code here.
